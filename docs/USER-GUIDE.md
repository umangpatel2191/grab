# GRAB user guide

GRAB turns any website into a brand kit inside Figma: every image, icon and SVG, the brand
colors, the fonts and type scale, and one-click brand guidelines.

## 1. Scan a website
1. Open **Plugins → GRAB** in Figma.
2. Paste one or more website links (one per line, up to 10).
3. Choose **Pages per site**: *Just this page*, *Up to 10/25/50/100*, or *Whole site*.
   GRAB follows the menu, footer and sitemap, and shows each picture once.
4. Optional: **Deep scan** opens every page in a browser to also find images that scripts add
   later. It is slower and only shown when the server supports it.
5. Press **Fetch**. Images appear within seconds; colors and fonts follow.

> The free server sleeps when nobody uses it. The first scan after a pause can take up to a
> minute while it wakes; GRAB retries on its own.

## 2. Browse and preview
- Filter with the chips: **Colors, Fonts, Images, Icons, GIFs, Videos**, or search (`/`).
- Hover a picture: **👁 View** (large preview), **＋ Add to Figma**, **↗ Open link**.
- In the preview: ← → to browse, **Space** to select, **Esc** to close; Download or Add to Figma.
- **HD** badge: a full-size original exists and is used for downloads and inserts.

## 3. Take what you need
- Select pictures (click), then use the bar at the bottom: **Copy** links, **Save**/**ZIP**,
  or **Add to Figma** (laid out in a frame named after the site).
- **Brand colors → Add to Figma:** swatches plus local color styles.
- **Fonts → Add to Figma:** a type specimen plus text styles (fonts Figma has, including all
  Google Fonts).

## 4. Brand guidelines and tokens
In the **Brand kit** bar at the top of the results:
- **Create Brand Book:** a 16:9 guidelines deck in Figma (cover, contents, logo and variations,
  color, typography, type scale, iconography, imagery) plus color variables, color styles, text
  styles and icon components.
- **Export tokens:** a zip with `tokens.json` (W3C design tokens, works with Tokens Studio) and a
  Tailwind theme.

## 5. Your last scan is kept
Close GRAB and reopen it: your last scan is still there. **New scan** shows a *Last scan* card to
go back. Starting a new scan replaces it.

## Settings
- **HD images:** full-size originals; small images enlarged 2× for Figma and downloads.
- **Theme:** follow Figma, light or dark.
- **Clear recent links.**

## Good to know
- Sites that block automated tools, or show content only after login, can only be partly read.
- Video files up to 50 MB can be saved; streams and embeds open at the source and Figma gets the
  poster image.
