# GRAB privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

GRAB is a Figma plugin that collects images, icons, colors and fonts from websites you choose.
This page explains what it does with data.

## What GRAB sends, and where
- **The website links you paste** are sent to the GRAB server
  (`https://grab-server-lz29.onrender.com`), which reads those public pages and returns what it finds.
- **Files** (images, icons, video posters) are fetched by the server **only when you preview,
  download or add them to Figma**, and passed straight to the plugin.
- GRAB does **not** send your Figma files, layers, account details, or anything else from Figma.

## What GRAB keeps
- **On the server:** a scan is kept in memory for up to 3 hours so previews and downloads work,
  then deleted. The server keeps no database, no accounts, and no analytics. Hosting logs
  (IP address, time, request path) are kept by the hosting provider for a short time for
  security and debugging.
- **In Figma on your computer** (Figma's plugin storage): your settings, your recent links, and
  your last scan, so it is still there when you reopen GRAB. Starting a new scan replaces it;
  "Clear recent links" in Settings removes the list.

## What GRAB does not do
- No login or account, no tracking, no ads, no selling or sharing of data.
- It reads only public pages. It does not bypass logins, paywalls, captchas or bot checks, and
  it follows each site's robots.txt.

## Content you collect
Images, logos, fonts and other content belong to their owners. GRAB is for reference and
inspiration; check the rights before using anything in your own work.
